# Community code of conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions and other AurumAI
community spaces. Be constructive, acknowledge uncertainty and focus technical
criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, hateful or discriminatory language, unwanted sexual content,
publication of another person's private information, and sustained disruption are
unacceptable. Respect confidentiality and do not pressure others to share personal
information or credentials.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, pause participation or restrict access to
community spaces. They consider context and the effect on other participants;
technical disagreement by itself is not misconduct.

Report sensitive incidents through an available verified private maintainer
channel or the hosting platform's reporting tools. Do not post private details
in a public issue. Maintainers must establish a verified private reporting path
when the public community repository is launched.

This is AurumAI's project policy. It does not replace a hosting platform's rules
or transfer ownership of third-party policy documents.
